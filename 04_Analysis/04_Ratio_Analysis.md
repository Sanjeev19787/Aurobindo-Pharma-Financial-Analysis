# Ratio Analysis

## Overview

Ratio analysis evaluates the financial performance and position of a company using relationships between different financial statement items.

## Company

Aurobindo Pharma Ltd.

## Study Period

FY2015–FY2026

## 1. Liquidity Ratios

Liquidity ratios measure the company's ability to meet its short-term obligations.

| Ratio | FY2015 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Current Ratio | 2.65 | 2.82 |
| Quick Ratio | 1.56 | 1.84 |

### Interpretation

The current ratio and quick ratio remained above 1 during the period, indicating the company's short-term liquidity position.

---

## 2. Solvency Ratios

Solvency ratios examine the company's long-term financial structure and reliance on debt.

| Ratio | FY2015 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Debt-Equity Ratio | 0.86 | 0.21 |
| Debt-to-Assets Ratio | 34.48% | 13.87% |

### Interpretation

The decline in the debt-equity ratio and debt-to-assets ratio indicates a reduction in the relative proportion of debt in the company's financial structure.

---

## 3. Profitability Ratios

Profitability ratios measure the company's ability to generate profits from its operations and resources.

| Ratio | FY2015 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Operating Profit Margin | 21.31% | 20.41% |
| Net Profit Margin | 12.98% | 10.41% |

### Interpretation

Operating profit margin remained relatively close to its FY2015 level, while net profit margin was lower in FY2026 compared with FY2015.

---

## 4. Return Ratios

Return ratios measure the returns generated from the company's assets and shareholders' equity.

| Ratio | FY2016 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| ROE | 32.53% | 9.93% |
| ROA | 14.05% | 6.51% |

### Interpretation

ROE and ROA declined over the period shown, indicating changes in the relationship between the company's profits, assets and shareholders' equity.

---

## 5. Efficiency Ratios

Efficiency ratios examine how effectively the company uses its assets and manages its operating resources.

| Ratio | FY2016 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Asset Turnover | 0.96 | 0.63 |
| Receivables Turnover | 3.38 | 5.24 |

### Interpretation

Asset turnover declined from FY2016 to FY2026, while receivables turnover increased during the same period.

---

## 6. Earnings Per Share

| Metric | FY2015 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| EPS | ₹26.98 | ₹60.34 |

### Interpretation

EPS increased over the study period, indicating an increase in earnings attributable to each share.

---

## Overall Interpretation

The ratio analysis provides a broad view of Aurobindo Pharma's liquidity, solvency, profitability, returns and operating efficiency across the study period.
