# DuPont Analysis

## Overview

DuPont Analysis breaks Return on Equity (ROE) into three components to understand the factors contributing to shareholder returns.

## Company

Aurobindo Pharma Ltd.

## Study Period

FY2024–FY2026

## Formula

ROE = Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier

Where:

- Net Profit Margin measures profitability.
- Asset Turnover measures how efficiently assets are used to generate sales.
- Equity Multiplier reflects the relationship between total assets and shareholders' equity.

## DuPont Analysis

| Financial Year | Net Profit Margin | Asset Turnover | Equity Multiplier | ROE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| FY2024 | 10.93% | 0.69 | 1.49 | 11.18% |
| FY2025 | 10.98% | 0.67 | 1.51 | 11.15% |
| FY2026 | 10.41% | 0.63 | 1.53 | 9.93% |

## Interpretation

### FY2024

The ROE was 11.18%, supported by a net profit margin of 10.93%, asset turnover of 0.69 and an equity multiplier of 1.49.

### FY2025

ROE was 11.15%. Net profit margin increased slightly to 10.98%, while asset turnover declined to 0.67 and the equity multiplier increased to 1.51.

### FY2026

ROE declined to 9.93%. Net profit margin decreased to 10.41% and asset turnover declined further to 0.63, while the equity multiplier increased to 1.53.

## Overall Interpretation

The DuPont analysis shows that changes in ROE were associated with changes in profitability, asset utilisation and the equity multiplier.

From FY2024 to FY2026, net profit margin and asset turnover declined, while the equity multiplier increased. The combined effect resulted in ROE declining from 11.18% in FY2024 to 9.93% in FY2026.
