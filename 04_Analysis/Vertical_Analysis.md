# Vertical Analysis

## Overview

Vertical analysis expresses each financial statement item as a percentage of a common base, allowing the financial structure of the company to be examined across different years.

## Company

Aurobindo Pharma Ltd.

## Study Period

FY2015–FY2026

## Key Findings

### Profitability

- Operating Profit represented approximately 21.31% of sales in FY2015.
- Operating Profit represented approximately 20.41% of sales in FY2026.
- Net Profit represented approximately 12.98% of sales in FY2015.
- Net Profit represented approximately 10.41% of sales in FY2026.

### Financial Structure

- Reserves increased from approximately 39.71% of total assets in FY2015 to approximately 65.01% in FY2026.
- Borrowings decreased from approximately 34.48% of total assets in FY2015 to approximately 13.87% in FY2026.

## Interpretation

The vertical analysis shows changes in the composition of Aurobindo Pharma's financial statements over the study period. The proportion of reserves increased substantially, while borrowings represented a smaller proportion of total assets in FY2026 compared with FY2015.

The profitability percentages also changed over the study period, indicating variations in the relationship between profits and sales.
