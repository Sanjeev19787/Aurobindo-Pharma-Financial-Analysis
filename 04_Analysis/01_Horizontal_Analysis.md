# Horizontal Analysis

## Overview

Horizontal analysis examines changes in financial statement items across multiple financial years.

## Company

Aurobindo Pharma Ltd.

## Study Period

FY2015–FY2026

## Purpose

The analysis evaluates year-to-year changes in major financial statement items such as sales, operating profit and net profit.

## Key Findings

- Sales increased substantially over the study period.
- Sales experienced a decline in FY2022 before recovering in subsequent years.
- Operating profit increased over the study period.
- Net profit also increased over the long term, although annual performance varied.

## Interpretation

The horizontal analysis indicates overall long-term growth in the company's financial performance, while also showing fluctuations across individual financial years.
