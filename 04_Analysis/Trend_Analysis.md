# Trend Analysis

## Overview

Trend analysis examines the direction of financial performance over a period of time by taking a selected base year as 100.

## Company

Aurobindo Pharma Ltd.

## Study Period

FY2015–FY2026

## Base Year

FY2015 = 100

## Key Findings

| Financial Item | FY2026 Trend Index |
|---|---:|
| Sales | 278.06 |
| Operating Profit | 266.38 |
| Net Profit | 222.98 |
| Total Assets | 450.78 |
| Reserves | 737.92 |
| Borrowings | 181.37 |

## Interpretation

Using FY2015 as the base year, the trend analysis shows substantial growth in the company's financial position over the study period.

- Sales increased to 278.06, indicating that sales were approximately 2.78 times the FY2015 level.
- Operating Profit increased to 266.38.
- Net Profit increased to 222.98.
- Total Assets increased significantly to 450.78.
- Reserves showed the highest increase, reaching 737.92.
- Borrowings increased to 181.37, but at a lower rate than total assets and reserves.

Overall, the trend analysis highlights significant long-term changes in the company's financial performance and financial position.
