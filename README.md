# 📊 Financial Analysis of Aurobindo Pharma Ltd.

### Financial Reporting & Analysis | MBA | GITAM School of Business

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a financial analysis of **Aurobindo Pharma Ltd.** covering the financial years **FY2015–FY2026**.

The project applies multiple financial statement analysis techniques to understand the company's financial performance, financial position, profitability, liquidity, solvency, efficiency and return on equity.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- To analyze the financial statements of Aurobindo Pharma Ltd.
- To examine changes in financial performance over the study period.
- To evaluate the company's financial position using different analytical techniques.
- To analyze liquidity, solvency, profitability and efficiency.
- To understand the components contributing to Return on Equity using DuPont Analysis.
- To develop practical skills in Excel-based financial analysis and interpretation.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project uses the following financial analysis techniques:

### 1. Horizontal Analysis

Examines year-to-year changes in financial statement items to identify growth, decline and fluctuations over time.

### 2. Vertical Analysis

Expresses financial statement items as percentages of a common base to understand the composition of the financial statements.

### 3. Trend Analysis

Uses FY2015 as the base year (100) to examine the long-term direction of major financial indicators.

### 4. Ratio Analysis

The ratio analysis covers:

- Liquidity Ratios
- Solvency Ratios
- Profitability Ratios
- Return Ratios
- Efficiency Ratios
- Earnings Per Share

### 5. DuPont Analysis

DuPont Analysis decomposes Return on Equity into three components:

**ROE = Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier**

---

## 📈 Key Findings

Based on the financial analysis:

- Sales increased substantially over the study period.
- Operating profit and net profit increased over the long term.
- Total assets increased significantly during the study period.
- Reserves increased substantially as a proportion of total assets.
- Liquidity indicators remained above 1 during the period examined.
- Debt-equity and debt-to-assets ratios declined over the study period.
- Operating profit margin remained relatively close to its FY2015 level.
- Net profit margin was lower in FY2026 compared with FY2015.
- Asset turnover declined over the period examined.
- Earnings per share increased from FY2015 to FY2026.
- DuPont analysis showed changes in ROE associated with profitability, asset utilisation and the equity multiplier.
